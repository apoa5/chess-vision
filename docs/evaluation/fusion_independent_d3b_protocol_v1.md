# D.3b preregistered independent-session evaluation, v1

Registered 2026-10-22, before any eligible new-session recognition outcomes. This is a separate evaluation-only cohort, **not the untouched final holdout**. Independent validation is pending. Five A.3 pilot sessions and renamed/recompressed copies cannot enter this cohort. Training or threshold/method tuning on this cohort makes it development data permanently; a fresh registered cohort is then required. Do not relabel an inspected cohort as independent again.

## Frozen experiment

[Protocol configuration](../../configs/experiments/fusion_independent_d3b_v1.json) freezes uniform-5 mean logits (primary accuracy candidate), mean probabilities (reliability comparator), and whole-board quality-selected single frame. Use unchanged D.1 3-second candidates, uniform selector, duplicate safeguards and opt-in marked-board geometry. Use unchanged B.2 tighter-224 checkpoint and D.3 formulas/parameters. Register code/config hashes and inputs **before running recognition or examining outcomes**. The [versioned preregistration](../../data/metadata/fusion_independent_d3b_preregistration_v1.json) freezes protocol and executable source hashes before any new cohort outcomes; registration refuses changed code/configuration. A change requires a newly versioned protocol, not silent edits. No oracle, per-square frame choice, threshold sweep for selection, chess repair or calibration fit. Preserve full distributions. D.3 diagnostic abstention is an exploratory probe, never automatic completion.

Plan at least **ten new positions in ten sessions**, captured on at least two dates, with diffuse and directional lighting represented. Ten is an engineering minimum to begin an audit, not a statistical guarantee. Include crowded boards, sparse endgames and pawn/bishop/knight/rook/queen confusions. Prefer a second board/set or phone where available; record the actual equipment rather than claiming variation that did not occur. All primary positions must be distinct from the pilot/training placements. Record shared setup, piece set and environment as correlation groups. Repeated takes of a physical position remain one position group, never independent positions; report both session and grouped-position outcomes.

## Practical capture checklist

1. Reserve new session/position/position-group IDs and an evaluation-only directory, e.g. `data/raw/d3b_independent_01/<session_id>/`. Keep videos outside Git; use original native phone files or A.3 guided timestamped sequences. Do not transfer via a compressing messenger. Preserve originals plus SHA-256, native metadata/index and all decoded PTS/time bases.
2. Set the physical position. Reuse an exact FEN/reference already supplied if applicable; annotate only missing information. Exact repeated pilot/training positions are useful development tests but cannot serve as new-position independent validation. Take a clear reference photo immediately before recording. Record the complete FEN, label-version/provenance and its photo hash. Human confirmation must attest photo/FEN agreement and that no piece moved throughout the video; unknown/pending is not verified ground truth.
3. Make one continuous approximately 60–90 second tour, without moving board or pieces. Cover White-near, White-right, Black-near and White-left; traverse moderate and higher elevations, with brief steady views but continuous recording. Record missing coverage, motion blur, cropped borders and changed lighting honestly. Preserve transitions and failed views. Do not fabricate precise poses: side/elevation notes may be coarse estimates or unknown.
4. Record device/lens/camera mode when available, actual lighting/environment/board/set IDs and capture date. Native metadata must include codec, encoded/display resolution, frame-rate information, duration, frame count, and an ordered actual-PTS index. Variable rate must not be replaced with frame-index/nominal-fps timestamps.
5. Reuse native ingestion/indexing (`ingest_native_pilot.py`, `validate_native_pilot.py`), D.1 candidate/selection machinery and D.2 review/extraction contracts in fresh run directories. The old HD-vs-Telegram comparison runner has pilot-specific secondary paths: **do not point it at a new cohort and assume it is a generic raw-video importer**. Supply a newly registered A.3 native catalog and native metadata artifacts; never edit the old pilot catalog or reports. The D.3b entry point consumes the reviewed D.2 evidence contract, not raw MOV files.
6. Review all five selected frames per session: true playing corners, semantic orientation, canonical a1–h8 map and sufficient crop alignment. Reuse hash-bound D.2 review packets; explicit human attribution is mandatory. Keep rejected/unresolved/completed-unverified distinct. Do not silently substitute a better frame. Fewer than five eligible frames makes the primary session unusable; count it against the registered exact-board denominator and report missingness.
7. Complete the registry template and run `register` before classifier extraction/outcome inspection. The lock hashes originals, reference and native metadata, ground-truth attestations, protocol, source code and model configuration. Include the resulting `independent_registration_sha256` and `independent_protocol_sha256` in the new D.2 packet configuration before extraction. The evaluator requires these bindings. Hashes ensure reproducibility; human provenance/novelty assertions still require honest review.
8. Run unchanged D.2 extraction once, saving full logits/probabilities, then the command below. Preserve every registered session, including unusable recordings. No random frame split. Multiple representations or takes of the same physical position stay grouped; do not count compressed copies twice.

## Runnable contract and commands

The committed [registry template](../../configs/capture/fusion_independent_d3b_registry_template_v1.json) intentionally contains **zero collected sessions** and null paths in its example; it does not invent captures or labels. Create a new registry by following its documented required fields. Existing references/labels may be linked by path/hash rather than copied or requested again.

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/evaluate_independent_fusion.py register --registry data/metadata/d3b_new_sessions.json --output outputs/scanning/d3b_independent_01_registration.json
# Build fresh reviewed D.2 evidence, binding the registration/protocol hashes in its packet config.
.venv/bin/python scripts/evaluate_independent_fusion.py evaluate --registration outputs/scanning/d3b_independent_01_registration.json --evidence-manifest data/metadata/d3b_independent_01_evidence.json --output-dir outputs/scanning/d3b_independent_01_evaluation
```

Registration is immutable and results require a fresh output directory. The evaluator refuses pilot IDs, source hashes, reused pilot placements or missing human truth/stability confirmation in independent mode, and checks source/model/crop/review/session bindings. An explicit `--functional-development` mode permits pilot-based tooling tests, always labeled development with independent status pending. It must be passed consistently to registration and evaluation. It does not certify new independent data.

## Outcomes and analysis unit

Report one board per registered session/method, exact numerator/registered denominator, conditional occupied and overall accuracy, per-class confusion, confidently wrong occupied/empty predictions (score ≥0.9), original D.3 withholding, worst-square uncertainty, board-mean confidence, disagreement and all-input support. Compare paired sessions across the three frozen methods; show every session and separate unavailable geometry. Report cohort metadata/coverage, unique position and setup counts, selection/geometry/inference costs from the evidence and fusion wall time. Repeated frames do not enlarge the session count.

Five-view comparisons are primary. Existing diversity-10 data is sensitivity only; its nonnested views cannot demonstrate that *additional* observations cause resolution. Any future nested extra-frame study must register its selection/order/budgets and source grouping before outcomes, report whether previously uncertain or wrong squares resolve versus become confidently wrong, and retain increased cost. Do not add frames until a known FEN is reconstructed.

## Conservative decision for beginning D.4 research

This is a **human research-review decision**, not implemented runtime scan acceptance. Before implementing D.4 require: the registered independent cohort is collected and fully accounted for; independently reviewed geometry/labels; paired exact-board improvements that are not explained by a single position or loss of a previously exact board; no unflagged high-confidence occupied errors in the cohort under a separately frozen proposed abstention rule; board-level withholding detects every observed incorrect board without withholding all correct boards; useful coverage/risk across sessions, classes and setups rather than pooled empty-square confidence; and a registered nested extra-frame experiment showing uncertainty actually resolves without confident regressions. Inspect every failure and report zero-error sample size honestly: zero observed unsafe accepts is **not proof of safety**. If evidence is inconsistent, revise offline methods using a development cohort and reserve a genuinely new independent cohort. The final holdout remains sealed. Ten sessions alone cannot establish deployment safety, nor calibrate a production threshold.
