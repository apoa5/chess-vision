# D.3 prespecified offline design

This design and `configs/experiments/fusion_d3.json` are recorded before D.3 predictions/evaluation. No parameter search, fitting, pilot-based temperature scaling or leave-session-out tuning is performed: five correlated development sessions are insufficient to justify it. No final holdout is used. Predictions are generated without loading FENs, corrected labels or D.2 oracle results; a separate evaluator loads the existing owner-approved c7 supplement afterward.

Compare the same five methods on uniform-5 (primary), diversity-5 (equal budget) and diversity-10 (actual 9/10/10/8/6 frames, not equal cost). Each square combines only the same session/position/source/canonical coordinate with compatible checkpoint, crop configuration and 13-class order. Preserve all source observations and hashes. Invalid observations are excluded with reasons; corrupt source bindings fail closed rather than silently changing the population. No frame is replaced.

Methods:

1. **single_quality:** select ONE frame for the whole board by maximum existing D.1 quality score; ties use earlier original timestamp, then frame ID. No per-square best-view selection. Its probability vector is unchanged.
2. **majority_vote:** per-class argmax vote proportions. Ties use the arithmetic mean probability among tied classes, then the lowest class ID. The vote distribution is a descriptive score, not a calibrated posterior.
3. **mean_probabilities:** arithmetic mean of the existing uncalibrated probability vectors, then argmax.
4. **mean_logits:** arithmetic mean of the original 13 float32-logit vectors in float64, followed by one stable softmax; no temperature, logit standardization or sum. Averaging, rather than summing, prevents identical repetition from sharpening scores. This is related to a normalized geometric mean, not a claim of independent likelihoods; logit magnitudes can still let confidently wrong views dominate.
5. **clustered_quality_median:** form deterministic chronological complete-link groups. A frame can join a group only when it is within the registered D.1 view distance <0.12 OR within 7 s of **every** member; choose the first admissible group. This prevents temporal single-link chains from merging an entire recording. Within each group compute the probability average weighted by clipped D.1 quality in [0.25,1]. Across groups take the componentwise median and renormalize. If all median coordinates are zero, fall back explicitly to equal-group arithmetic mean. Each group has equal intergroup influence, regardless of repeated frame count. Groups are dependence heuristics, never called independent measurements. D.1 quality is uncalibrated image quality, not recognition reliability.

All ties are deterministic. Classifier and geometry are never rerun. Methods 2–4 intentionally expose simple aggregation's correlation vulnerabilities; no naïve probability product or learned fusion model is added.

For every square retain the score distribution, entropy, margin, all input IDs/labels, vote counts/entropy, variation ratio, Jensen–Shannon disagreement, heuristic groups/support, contradictory strong-view flags and explicit abstention reasons. Contradictory strong views are different frame argmax labels each with probability ≥0.8. Such conflict always stays visible regardless of the fused argmax.

A **diagnostic square abstention** rule, not a D.4 scan-completion gate: score ≥0.8 and top-two margin ≥0.2; fused methods additionally require at least two supporting groups and ≥2/3 group-argmax support. Strong contradictory labels force abstention. The single-frame baseline has only its own confidence/margin check; it is not credited with multi-view support. Abstention withholds an operational label while retaining the tentative prediction for ordinary accuracy evaluation. No resolved-board/scan-complete flag is computed. These thresholds are prespecified engineering probes, not validated safety guarantees.

Report ordinary and selective accuracy/coverage, false-piece/missed-piece/identity errors, per-class confusion, exact piece-placement boards, high-score errors, NLL (floor 1e-12, zero true-score count), multiclass Brier and 10-bin ECE. Score-based calibration metrics do not make vote/median/softmax scores calibrated. No approved compatible B.4 artifact is present; do not invent a calibrated variant. Statistical units are the five sessions; square/frame counts are descriptive, with no IID significance claims.

Keep per-session comparisons against same-condition single_quality and uniform-5 mean_probabilities/majority_vote. Best-individual-frame-with-GT and D.2 oracle ceilings, if reported, stay in a separate diagnostic section. Never use them for prediction, weights or hyperparameters. The D.2 oracle is limited to selecting observed per-view argmax labels, not a mathematical bound on full-distribution fusion.

Legality is read-only, separate from accuracy: run the existing standard-chess validator with BOTH assumed sides to move, no castling/en-passant history, and clearly label assumptions. No legal repair, target-piece counts, move history, search, or GT-based correction of predictions. No production/browser changes, scan-completion implementation, training, or D.4 work.
